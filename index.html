Who We Are

A Community of Faith, Purpose & Service

Guiding Light CBO is a non-profit, non-political community-based organization committed to transforming the lives of young people through faith-based mentorship, legal empowerment, rehabilitation, and moral leadership development.

Anchored on Christian principles and values, the organization seeks to nurture a generation of young men and women modeled after Christ-centered leadership — marked by integrity, discipline, virtue, and service to society.

"No young person is beyond redemption. With proper mentorship, legal support, moral guidance, and community care, vulnerable youth can be redirected toward lives of dignity, leadership, and meaningful contribution."

Founded by a multidisciplinary team of advocates, educators, medical professionals, and community leaders, GL-CBO bridges the gap between justice, mentorship, and holistic youth development.

Legal Empowerment

Bridging the Justice Gap for Young People

When a young person is arrested, how the process unfolds can shape their entire future. Even brief remand without proper support exposes youth to hardened criminal environments, trauma, and further radicalization.

GL-CBO works closely with two partner law firms as our resident panel of advocates, alongside a network of pro bono advocates on call — providing personalized legal advisory services and representation for deserving youth cases.

We complement government initiatives by making justice accessible, personal, and restorative — not just procedural.

1

Early Intervention

We step in at first contact with the justice system, ensuring youth are not lost within complex legal processes.

2

Legal Advisory & Representation

Free legal counsel and where necessary, actual courtroom representation through our panel advocates and pro bono network.

3

Diversion & Restorative Justice

Facilitating diversion, mediation, and restorative justice initiatives that focus on healing rather than punishment.

4

Legal Literacy Programs

Empowering youth, families, and communities with knowledge of their rights, responsibilities, and the legal system.

5

Rehabilitation & Reintegration

Post-legal support ensuring youth return to education, community, and spiritual life with dignity and purpose.

Structure & Accountability

Governance & Legal Framework

Guiding Light CBO operates under a formal Constitution anchored in integrity, transparency, and accountability to the communities we serve.

📜

Non-Profit Status

A voluntary, non-profit, non-political, and non-sectarian Community Based Organization. All income and property applied solely toward organizational objectives.

🏛️

Board Leadership

Guided by a diverse non-legal board of respected community leaders providing societal, ethical, social, and holistic guidance across all programs and operations.

Legal Partnerships

Two partner law firms serve as resident panel advocates. A wider network of pro bono advocates handles legal advisory and representation for youth cases.
